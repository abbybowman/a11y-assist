ACT 36b590's examples are static pages with the error message already
shown; nothing on them detects an error, so under the TT's applicability
("automatic error detection") they are DOES NOT APPLY for a tool that
enters values. They are not vendored; the rule's expectations (identify the
field; describe the cause or the fix, visibly and in the accessibility tree)
are cited in the spec.
