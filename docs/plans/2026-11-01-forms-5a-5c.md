# Plan: 5.A label provided and 5.C programmatic label (2026-11-01)

Branch `forms-5a-5c-labels`, started from `forms-5f-5g-errors` (not merged
into main yet; this work reuses its `forms.py`). Specs:
[5a-label-provided.md](../rules/5a-label-provided.md) and
[5c-programmatic-label.md](../rules/5c-programmatic-label.md), both 0.2.0.
Both are the whole TT test, not only what scanners miss: every form
component the page has, the visual side (5.A) and the programmatic side
(5.C, from ANDI's output, as the PDF prescribes and a Trusted Tester
reads it). Scanners (axe-core, HTML_CodeSniffer) are not
vendored: their form rules check the same names ANDI computes
(coverage map), and Abby chose not to vendor them first.
